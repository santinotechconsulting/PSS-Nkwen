# Content Collection Checklist for PSS Nkwen Administration
**Prepared by Santino Tech Consulting - "We Build Your Digital Presence"**

Before the official public launch of the PSS Nkwen website, the school management and Presbyterian Education Authority are requested to supply and confirm the following official assets and institutional data:

---

## 1. Visual & Institutional Identity
- [ ] **Official School Crest / Logo**: High-resolution PNG or SVG vector file of the official school crest.
- [ ] **Official School Colors**: Formal color specifications (Hex/RGB/Pantone). Currently using provisional Navy `#0F2544` and Gold `#C59B27`.
- [ ] **Official Motto**: Formal school motto (the website currently uses the proposed copy: *"Learning, Character and Service"*).
- [ ] **Founding Year & History**: Exact founding year and narrative of the school's foundation under the Presbyterian Church in Cameroon.

---

## 2. Executive Leadership & Governance
- [ ] **Principal's Profile**: Full name, qualifications, official portrait photograph, and formal welcome address to prospective students and parents.
- [ ] **Senior Administration**: Names and designations of Vice-Principals, Chaplains, Dean of Studies, and Discipline Masters.
- [ ] **Presbyterian Education Authority Liaison**: Official desk or coordinator details.

---

## 3. Communication & Contact Verification
- [ ] **Current Official Telephone Number**: Confirmation of the directory-listed number `677866377` or submission of new primary office lines.
- [ ] **Official Email Addresses**: Institutional email address (e.g. `info@pssnkwen.edu.cm` or `principal@pssnkwen.edu.cm`).
- [ ] **Physical Street / Postal Address**: Exact postal address (P.O. Box), physical landmarks in Nkwen, Bamenda.
- [ ] **Official Social Media Handles**: Verified links to official school Facebook page or YouTube channel (if operational).

---

## 4. Academic & Curriculum Data
- [ ] **Full List of Classes & Streams**: Complete listing of Form 1 through Upper Sixth classes and stream divisions (Science, Arts, Commercial).
- [ ] **Department Heads**: Confirmed list of academic departments and respective subject coordinators.
- [ ] **Official Academic Calendar**: Current term dates, mid-term breaks, public holidays, and internal examination timetables.

---

## 5. Admissions, Fees & Official Forms
- [ ] **Admission Deadlines**: Official deadlines for entrance examinations, Form 1 registrations, and transfer applications.
- [ ] **School Fees Schedule**: Approved fee structures for day students and boarders (if boarding is operated), PTA contributions, and registration charges.
- [ ] **Official Admission Forms (PDF)**: Scanned or digital PDF copies of formal registration forms for prospective parents to download.

---

## 6. Campus Photographs & Media
- [ ] **Approved Campus Photographs**: High-resolution photos of classroom blocks, administrative building, library, science laboratories, school chapel, and sports grounds.
- [ ] **Photo Consent & Privacy**: Confirmation that photographs comply with Presbyterian Church in Cameroon child protection and public media guidelines.
